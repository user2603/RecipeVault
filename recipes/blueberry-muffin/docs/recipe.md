# Blueberry Muffins

Tall bakery-style muffins bursting with blueberries.

## Ingredients
- 250g plain flour
- 150g caster sugar
- 200g blueberries
- 125g melted butter
- 180ml milk
- 1 egg
- 2 teaspoons baking powder

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 200C and line a 12-hole muffin tin.
2. Whisk the milk, egg and butter together.
3. Fold the wet mix into the flour, sugar and baking powder - lumps are fine.
4. Gently fold in the blueberries.
5. Fill the cases and bake for 20 minutes until domed and golden.

## Yield
Makes 12 muffins
