# Soup Dumplings

Pleated parcels hiding a burst of rich pork broth.

## Ingredients
- 300g plain flour plus more for dusting
- 160ml hot water
- 300g minced pork
- 200g chilled pork aspic, diced
- 1 tablespoon grated ginger
- 2 spring onions, minced
- Black vinegar and ginger, for dipping

## Instructions
1. Mix the flour with the hot water and knead 10 minutes; rest 1 hour.
2. Mix the pork with ginger, spring onion and the diced aspic.
3. Roll wrappers thin at the edges.
4. Pleat 16 dumplings tightly.
5. Steam in a lined basket for 8 minutes and serve immediately.

## Yield
Makes 16 dumplings
