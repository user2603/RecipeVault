# Guacamole

Chunky avocado mash with lime, jalapeno and lots of coriander.

## Ingredients
- 3 ripe avocados
- 1 lime, juiced
- Half a small red onion, finely diced
- 1 jalapeno, finely chopped
- A handful of coriander, chopped
- Salt

## Instructions
1. Mash the avocados roughly, keeping some chunks.
2. Fold through the onion, jalapeno and coriander.
3. Add the lime juice and salt to taste.
4. Press cling film onto the surface until serving.

## Yield
Makes about 2 cups
