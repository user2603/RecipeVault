# Avocado Toast

Crushed avocado on golden sourdough with lemon and chili flakes.

## Ingredients
- 2 ripe avocados
- 4 thick slices sourdough bread
- Juice of half a lemon
- Half a teaspoon chili flakes
- Flaky salt and black pepper

## Instructions
1. Toast the sourdough until deeply golden.
2. Crush the avocado with the lemon juice, salt and pepper.
3. Pile the avocado generously onto the toast.
4. Finish with chili flakes and a little more salt.

## Yield
Serves 2
