# Chocolate Chip Cookies

Chewy-centred cookies with crisp edges and pools of dark chocolate.

## Ingredients
- 250g plain flour
- 150g softened butter
- 150g light brown sugar
- 1 egg plus 1 yolk
- 200g dark chocolate chips
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Half a teaspoon baking soda

## Instructions
1. Cream the butter and sugar until pale, then beat in the egg, yolk and vanilla.
2. Stir in the flour, baking soda and chocolate chips.
3. Chill the dough for 30 minutes.
4. Scoop balls onto trays and bake at 180C for 11 minutes.
5. Leave to firm up on the tray before moving.

## Yield
Makes 24 cookies
