# Miso Soup

Gentle dashi with silken tofu, wakame and scallion.

## Ingredients
- 800ml dashi stock
- 3 tablespoons white miso paste
- 200g silken tofu, diced
- 1 tablespoon dried wakame
- 2 spring onions, sliced

## Instructions
1. Rehydrate the wakame in cold water for 5 minutes.
2. Warm the dashi without boiling.
3. Dissolve the miso in a ladleful of broth, then stir it back in.
4. Add the tofu and wakame and warm through.
5. Top with spring onion.

## Yield
Serves 2
