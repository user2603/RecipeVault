# Pulled Pork Sandwiches

Slow-shredded shoulder piled on buns with slaw and sauce.

## Ingredients
- 1.5kg pork shoulder
- 3 tablespoons brown sugar rub
- 250ml BBQ sauce plus more to serve
- 6 brioche buns
- Half a white cabbage, shredded
- 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
- Lemon juice

## Instructions
1. Rub the shoulder all over and cook covered at 140C for 6 hours.
2. Rest 30 minutes, then shred with two forks.
3. Toss the shredded pork with warm BBQ sauce.
4. Dress the cabbage with mayo and lemon for a quick slaw.
5. Toast the buns and load with pork and slaw.

## Yield
Serves 6
