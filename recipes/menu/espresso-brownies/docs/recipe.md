# Espresso Brownies

Dense fudgy brownies sharpened with a double shot of espresso.

## Ingredients
- 200g dark chocolate
- 180g butter
- 3 eggs
- 200g sugar
- 1 shot of espresso
- 100g plain flour
- 2 tablespoons cocoa

## Instructions
1. Melt the chocolate and butter together.
2. Whisk the eggs, sugar and espresso until moussey.
3. Fold the two together.
4. Fold in the flour and cocoa - stop as soon as it combines.
5. Bake in a lined tin at 180C for 25 minutes; the middle should wobble.

## Yield
Makes 16 squares
