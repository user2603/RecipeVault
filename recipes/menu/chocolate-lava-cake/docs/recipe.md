# Chocolate Lava Cakes

Individual cakes with molten centres - the timing is everything.

## Ingredients
- 200g dark chocolate
- 150g butter
- 4 eggs
- 150g sugar
- 60g plain flour
- Cocoa, for dusting
- Vanilla ice cream, to serve

## Instructions
1. Melt the chocolate and butter together.
2. Whisk the eggs and sugar until thick.
3. Fold in the flour, then the chocolate mix.
4. Butter and cocoa-dust 4 ramekins and fill.
5. Bake at 220C for exactly 11 minutes - the edges set, the middle stays liquid.

## Yield
Makes 4 cakes
