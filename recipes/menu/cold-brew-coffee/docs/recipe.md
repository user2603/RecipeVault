# Cold Brew Coffee

Steeped overnight for a sweet, smooth, low-acid concentrate.

## Ingredients
- 100g coarse-ground coffee
- 1 litre cold filtered water
- Milk or water, to serve

## Instructions
1. Combine the grounds and water in a big jar.
2. Stir once, cover and steep 16 hours at room temperature.
3. Strain through a paper filter - do not squeeze.
4. Chill and serve over ice, cut 50/50 with milk or water.

## Yield
Makes about 4 glasses
