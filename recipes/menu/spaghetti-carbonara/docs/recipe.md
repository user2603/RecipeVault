# Spaghetti Carbonara

The Roman classic - pancetta, egg, pecorino and nothing else.

## Ingredients
- 400g spaghetti
- 150g pancetta, diced
- 3 egg yolks plus 1 whole egg
- 60g grated pecorino romano
- Freshly ground black pepper

## Instructions
1. Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water.
2. Crisp the pancetta in a wide pan, then kill the heat.
3. Whisk the eggs, pecorino and plenty of pepper.
4. Toss the drained pasta in the pancetta pan off the heat.
5. Add the egg mixture with a splash of pasta water and toss to a glossy sauce.

## Yield
Serves 2
