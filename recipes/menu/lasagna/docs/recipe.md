# Sunday Lasagna

Layers of slow-cooked beef ragu, bechamel and pasta baked until bubbling.

## Ingredients
- 12 lasagna sheets
- 500g minced beef
- 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 800g tomato passata
- 500ml bechamel sauce
- 200g mozzarella, torn
- 50g grated parmesan

## Instructions
1. Brown the beef with the onion and garlic, then simmer in the passata for 40 minutes.
2. Spread a spoonful of ragu over the base of a dish.
3. Layer pasta, ragu, bechamel and cheese three times.
4. Top with mozzarella and parmesan.
5. Bake at 190C for 45 minutes until golden.

## Yield
Serves 6
