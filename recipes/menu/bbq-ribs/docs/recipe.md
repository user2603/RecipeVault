# Sticky BBQ Ribs

Low-slow racks glazed in sticky sauce until they bend but not break.

## Ingredients
- 2 racks pork ribs
- 2 tablespoons smoked paprika
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 300ml BBQ sauce
- Cider vinegar spray

## Instructions
1. Skin the membrane off the back of the racks.
2. Rub with the paprika, sugar and garlic and rest overnight.
3. Smoke or bake at 130C for 3 hours, spritzing with vinegar.
4. Brush with sauce and return for 30 minutes.
5. Rest 15 minutes and cut between the bones.

## Yield
Serves 4
