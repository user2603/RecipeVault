# Caprese Salad

Tomatoes, creamy mozzarella and basil dressed with olive oil.

## Ingredients
- 4 ripe tomatoes, thickly sliced
- 250g fresh mozzarella, sliced
- Fresh basil leaves
- 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- Flaky salt and black pepper

## Instructions
1. Alternate tomato and mozzarella slices across a platter.
2. Tuck basil leaves between the slices.
3. Drizzle with the olive oil.
4. Finish with flaky salt and plenty of black pepper.

## Yield
Serves 2
