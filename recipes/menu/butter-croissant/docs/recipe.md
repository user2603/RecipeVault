# Butter Croissants

Shattering, honeycombed lamination with nothing but patience and butter.

## Ingredients
- 500g strong flour
- 60g sugar
- 10g salt
- 10g instant yeast
- 290ml cold milk
- 280g cold butter, in a slab
- 1 egg, for glazing

## Instructions
1. Make a soft dough and chill overnight.
2. Lock the butter slab inside and give 3 letter folds, resting between each.
3. Roll to 4mm and cut long triangles.
4. Roll up firmly, curve the ends and prove 2 hours.
5. Glaze and bake at 200C for 15 minutes until deep gold.

## Yield
Makes 8 croissants
