# Coconut Curry

A quick weeknight curry - velvety coconut milk over golden chicken.

## Ingredients
- 600g chicken thighs, chunked
- 3 tablespoons red curry paste
- 2 cans coconut milk
- 1 tablespoon fish sauce
- 1 tablespoon palm or brown sugar
- 1 aubergine, cubed
- Rice, to serve

## Instructions
1. Fry the curry paste in a little oil until fragrant.
2. Add the chicken and turn to coat.
3. Pour in the coconut milk, fish sauce and sugar.
4. Add the aubergine and simmer 20 minutes.
5. Serve over rice.

## Yield
Serves 4
