# Tomato Bruschetta

Grilled bread topped with a bright tomato and basil mixture.

## Ingredients
- 1 baguette, sliced
- 4 ripe tomatoes, diced
- A handful of fresh basil leaves
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 splash of balsamic vinegar

## Instructions
1. Grill the baguette slices until charred at the edges.
2. Toss the tomatoes with torn basil, garlic, oil and vinegar.
3. Season generously with salt and pepper.
4. Spoon the tomato mixture over the grilled bread just before serving.

## Yield
Serves 4
