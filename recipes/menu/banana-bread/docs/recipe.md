# Banana Bread

Moist, dark banana bread with toasted walnuts - the house favourite.

## Ingredients
- 3 very ripe bananas
- 125g melted butter
- 150g brown sugar
- 1 egg, beaten
- 200g plain flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 75g walnuts, toasted

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 175C and line a loaf tin.
2. Mash the bananas and mix with the butter, sugar and egg.
3. Fold in the flour, baking soda and walnuts.
4. Bake for 55 minutes until a skewer comes out clean.

## Yield
1 loaf
