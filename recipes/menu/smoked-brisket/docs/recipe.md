# Low and Slow Smoked Brisket

Twelve hours of smoke and rest for slicing-brisket at its best.

## Ingredients
- 1 whole beef brisket, about 5kg
- 100g coarse black pepper
- 80g kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- Oak wood chunks, for smoke

## Instructions
1. Trim the fat cap to about 1cm.
2. Season heavily with the salt, pepper and garlic powder the night before.
3. Smoke at 110C, oak chunks on the coals, about 10 hours.
4. Wrap at the stall and probe until 95C.
5. Rest, wrapped, for 2 hours before slicing against the grain.

## Yield
Serves 8
