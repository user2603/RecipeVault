# Egg Fried Rice

Day-old rice tossed fast with egg, peas and spring onion.

## Ingredients
- 400g cold cooked rice
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 100g frozen peas
- 3 spring onions, sliced
- 2 tablespoons light soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil

## Instructions
1. Scramble the eggs in a very hot wok and set aside.
2. Add the rice and stir-fry until piping hot and separated.
3. Toss in the peas and spring onion.
4. Return the egg, add the soy and sesame oil and toss once more.

## Yield
Serves 2
