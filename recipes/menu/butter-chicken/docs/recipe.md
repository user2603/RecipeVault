# Butter Chicken

Charred chicken in a glossy tomato-cream gravy with fenugreek.

## Ingredients
- 700g chicken thighs
- 200g yogurt
- 2 tablespoons tandoori spice
- 800g tomato passata
- 80g butter
- 150ml cream
- 1 teaspoon dried fenugreek leaves
- Ginger and garlic

## Instructions
1. Marinate the chicken in yogurt and spices overnight.
2. Char the pieces under a hot grill.
3. Simmer passata with ginger, garlic and butter for 15 minutes.
4. Blend smooth, add the chicken and cream.
5. Crush in the fenugreek and simmer 10 minutes.

## Yield
Serves 4
