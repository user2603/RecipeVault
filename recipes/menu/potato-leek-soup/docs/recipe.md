# Potato and Leek Soup

Silky, humble vichyssoise-adjacent soup finished with chives.

## Ingredients
- 3 leeks, washed and sliced
- 500g floury potatoes, diced
- 1 litre chicken stock
- 50g butter
- 100ml single cream
- Chives, snipped

## Instructions
1. Melt the butter and sweat the leeks until soft and sweet.
2. Add the potatoes and stock and simmer 20 minutes.
3. Blend until completely silky.
4. Stir in the cream, season well and finish with chives.

## Yield
Serves 4
