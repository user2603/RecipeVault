# Crispy Falafel

Herby chickpea fritters, crisp outside and green within.

## Ingredients
- 400g soaked dried chickpeas
- 1 large bunch parsley
- 1 bunch coriander
- 2 garlic cloves
- 2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- Oil, for shallow frying

## Instructions
1. Blitz the chickpeas with the herbs, garlic and cumin to a coarse paste.
2. Stir in the baking soda and rest for 20 minutes.
3. Shape into small, flat rounds.
4. Fry in hot oil for 3 minutes per side until deeply crisp.
5. Drain on paper and salt lightly.

## Yield
Makes about 16 falafel
