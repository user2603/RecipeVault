# Grilled Street Corn

Charred corn brushed with lime butter and dusted with chili and cheese.

## Ingredients
- 4 ears of corn
- 50g butter
- 1 lime
- Chili powder
- 50g cotija or feta
- Chopped coriander

## Instructions
1. Grill the corn over high heat, turning, until charred in patches.
2. Melt the butter with the lime juice.
3. Brush the corn heavily with the lime butter.
4. Roll in the cheese, dust with chili and scatter coriander.

## Yield
4 ears
