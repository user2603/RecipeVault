# Beef Wellington

Fillet coated in mushroom duxelles and baked in shining pastry.

## Ingredients
- 600g beef fillet
- 300g mushrooms, finely chopped
- 4 slices prosciutto
- 500g puff pastry
- 2 egg yolks, beaten
- 1 tablespoon mustard
- Thyme

## Instructions
1. Sear the fillet on all sides, brush with mustard and chill.
2. Cook the mushrooms dry with thyme to a thick duxelles and cool.
3. Wrap the beef in prosciutto spread with duxelles.
4. Encase in pastry, seal and glaze with yolk.
5. Bake at 200C for 35 minutes for blushing pink, then rest 15.

## Yield
Serves 4
