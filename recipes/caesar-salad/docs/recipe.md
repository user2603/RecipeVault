# Caesar Salad

Crunchy romaine, garlicky dressing, sourdough croutons and shaved parmesan.

## Ingredients
- 2 romaine hearts
- 2 slices sourdough, cubed
- 50g grated parmesan
- 1 egg yolk
- 2 anchovy fillets
- 1 garlic clove
- Half a lemon

## Instructions
1. Fry the bread cubes in olive oil until crisp.
2. Mash the anchovy and garlic to a paste and whisk with the yolk and lemon.
3. Thin the dressing with olive oil and season.
4. Toss the romaine with the dressing, croutons and most of the parmesan.
5. Finish with the rest of the cheese.

## Yield
Serves 2
