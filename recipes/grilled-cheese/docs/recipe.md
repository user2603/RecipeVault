# Grilled Cheese Sandwich

Crisp buttered bread around molten sharp cheddar - simple perfection.

## Ingredients
- 2 thick slices sourdough bread
- A generous handful of grated sharp cheddar
- 2 tablespoons butter, softened

## Instructions
1. Butter the outside of both bread slices.
2. Heat a pan over low heat and lay in the first slice butter-side down.
3. Pile on the cheddar and top with the second slice.
4. Cook low and slow, flipping once, until golden and the cheese has fully melted.

## Yield
Serves 1
