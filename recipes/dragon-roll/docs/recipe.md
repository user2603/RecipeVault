# Dragon Roll

An inside-out sushi roll draped in avocado and lacquered with eel sauce.

## Ingredients
- 300g sushi rice
- 4 sheets nori
- 8 prawn tempura
- 2 avocados, sliced
- 1 cucumber, cut in batons
- Eel sauce
- Rice vinegar, sugar and salt

## Instructions
1. Season the warm rice with vinegar, sugar and salt.
2. Lay rice on nori, flip, and line tempura and cucumber along the middle.
3. Roll tight inside-out.
4. Drape avocado slices over the top and press a mat over.
5. Cut into 8, drizzle with eel sauce.

## Yield
Makes 8 pieces
