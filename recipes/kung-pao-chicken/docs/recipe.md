# Kung Pao Chicken

Fast wok chicken with peanuts, dried chilies and a numbing tingle.

## Ingredients
- 500g chicken thigh, cubed
- 75g roasted peanuts
- 6 dried chilies
- 2 spring onions, chunked
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon Chinkiang vinegar
- 1 teaspoon Sichuan peppercorns, crushed

## Instructions
1. Toss the chicken with a spoon of soy and cornflour.
2. Blast the chilies and peppercorns in hot oil for seconds.
3. Add the chicken and stir-fry until just cooked.
4. Add spring onion and peanuts.
5. Splash in soy and vinegar, toss twice and plate.

## Yield
Serves 2
