# Curry Laksa

Coconut-laksa noodle soup with prawns and tofu puffs.

## Ingredients
- 3 tablespoons laksa paste
- 400ml coconut milk
- 800ml chicken stock
- 200g prawns
- 6 tofu puffs, halved
- 2 nests egg noodles
- Bean sprouts and coriander
- Fried shallots

## Instructions
1. Fry the laksa paste in a little oil until it splits.
2. Add the stock and coconut milk and simmer.
3. Add the tofu puffs and warm 5 minutes.
4. Cook the noodles separately and divide into bowls.
5. Top with prawns poached in the broth, sprouts, coriander and shallots.

## Yield
Serves 2
