# Matcha Tiramisu

The Italian classic turned green - bitter matcha against mascarpone cream.

## Ingredients
- 300g mascarpone
- 3 eggs, separated
- 80g sugar
- 24 ladyfingers
- 3 tablespoons matcha
- Matcha, for dusting

## Instructions
1. Whisk the yolks and sugar, then beat in the mascarpone.
2. Fold in whisked egg whites to a cloud.
3. Brew the matcha strong and cool slightly.
4. Dip each ladyfinger briefly and layer in a dish.
5. Half cream, repeat, then chill 4 hours and dust thickly.

## Yield
Serves 6
