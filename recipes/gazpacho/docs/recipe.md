# Andalusian Gazpacho

Chilled tomato soup blitzed raw with peppers, cucumber and sherry vinegar.

## Ingredients
- 1kg ripe tomatoes
- Half a cucumber, roughly chopped
- 1 green pepper, seeded
- 50g day-old bread
- 2 tablespoons sherry vinegar
- 100ml olive oil

## Instructions
1. Soak the bread in a splash of water.
2. Blitz the tomatoes, cucumber, pepper, bread and vinegar until completely smooth.
3. With the motor running, stream in the olive oil.
4. Chill for at least 2 hours and season aggressively before serving.

## Yield
Serves 4
