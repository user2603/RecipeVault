# Onigiri

Warm salted rice triangles wrapped in nori with a hidden centre.

## Ingredients
- 400g sushi rice, freshly cooked
- 4 sheets nori, quartered
- 1 can tuna mixed with mayonnaise
- 4 umeboshi plums
- Salt

## Instructions
1. Wet your hands with salted water.
2. Press a handful of rice around a dab of filling.
3. Shape into triangles.
4. Wrap a strip of nori around the base of each one.

## Yield
Makes 8 onigiri
