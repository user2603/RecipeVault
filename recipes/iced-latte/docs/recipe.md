# Iced Latte

Cold milk over bold espresso, built backwards so the ice survives.

## Ingredients
- 2 shots of espresso
- 250ml cold milk
- A big handful of ice
- Sugar or syrup, optional

## Instructions
1. Fill a tall glass to the brim with ice.
2. Pour in the milk.
3. Sweeten now if you are going to.
4. Pull the espresso and pour it slowly over the back of a spoon.

## Yield
1 tall glass
