# Tacos al Pastor

Chili-and-achiote pork with charred pineapple on small corn tortillas.

## Ingredients
- 800g pork shoulder, sliced thin
- 3 tablespoons achiote paste
- 2 dried chilies, soaked
- 1 pineapple, cut in slabs
- 12 small corn tortillas
- White onion and coriander
- Limes

## Instructions
1. Blend the achiote, chilies, a splash of vinegar and salt.
2. Marinate the pork in it for at least 2 hours.
3. Grill the pineapple slabs until charred, then chop.
4. Griddle the pork hard until edged with black.
5. Warm the tortillas and build with pork, pineapple, onion, coriander and lime.

## Yield
Makes 12 tacos
