# Chicken Noodle Soup

A restorative classic with tender chicken, vegetables and egg noodles.

## Ingredients
- 2 chicken breasts
- 6 cups chicken stock
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 2 celery sticks, sliced
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cups egg noodles
- Fresh parsley, chopped

## Instructions
1. Simmer the chicken in the stock until cooked through, about 15 minutes.
2. Lift the chicken out and shred it.
3. Add the carrots, celery and onion to the stock and cook for 10 minutes.
4. Return the chicken with the noodles and simmer until tender.
5. Finish with parsley and season to taste.

## Yield
Serves 4
