# Stir-Fry Noodles

Fast wok noodles with crunchy peppers, ginger and spring onion.

## Ingredients
- 300g egg noodles
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- 2 carrots, julienned
- 3 spring onions, cut in batons
- 1 thumb ginger, grated
- 2 garlic cloves
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce

## Instructions
1. Cook the noodles a minute shy of the packet time and drain.
2. Blitz-fry the ginger and garlic in a hot wok.
3. Add the carrots and peppers and toss for 2 minutes.
4. Add the noodles and soy and toss until glossy.
5. Finish with the spring onion.

## Yield
Serves 2
