# Corn Chowder

Sweet corn and potato in a smoky bacon-cream broth.

## Ingredients
- 600g corn kernels
- 3 potatoes, diced
- 4 bacon rashers, chopped
- 1 onion, diced
- 500ml milk
- 150ml single cream

## Instructions
1. Fry the bacon until crisp and spoon out half for garnish.
2. Soften the onion in the bacon fat.
3. Add the potatoes, corn and milk and simmer 15 minutes.
4. Blend a third of the soup and stir back in with the cream.
5. Top with the reserved bacon.

## Yield
Serves 4
