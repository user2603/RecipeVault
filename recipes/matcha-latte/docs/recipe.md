# Matcha Latte

Frothy green tea whisked into hot milk with a little honey.

## Ingredients
- 1 teaspoon matcha powder
- 80ml just-boiled water
- 200ml steamed milk
- 1 teaspoon honey

## Instructions
1. Sift the matcha into a warm bowl.
2. Whisk with the water to a smooth, foamy paste.
3. Warm the milk and honey together.
4. Pour the milk over the matcha and whisk once more.

## Yield
1 mug
