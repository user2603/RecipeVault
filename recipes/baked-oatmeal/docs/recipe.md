# Baked Oatmeal with Blueberries

Warm banana-baked oatmeal studded with blueberries, perfect for a slow morning.

## Ingredients
- 2 cups rolled oats
- 2 ripe bananas
- 2 cups whole milk
- Quarter cup maple syrup
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 cup blueberries

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 180C.
2. Mash the bananas and whisk with the milk and maple syrup.
3. Fold in the oats, cinnamon and blueberries.
4. Pour into a buttered dish and bake for 35 minutes until set.

## Yield
Serves 4
