# Cinnamon Rolls

Soft buttery buns swirled with cinnamon sugar and iced while warm.

## Ingredients
- 500g strong flour
- 7g instant yeast
- 250ml warm milk
- 60g softened butter
- 80g sugar plus 60g brown for the filling
- 2 eggs
- 1 tablespoon cinnamon
- Icing sugar, for the glaze

## Instructions
1. Knead the flour, yeast, milk, butter, eggs and sugar for 8 minutes.
2. Rise until doubled, about an hour.
3. Roll out, spread with butter and scatter the brown sugar and cinnamon.
4. Roll up, slice into 12 and prove again in a tin.
5. Bake at 190C for 25 minutes and ice while warm.

## Yield
Makes 12 rolls
