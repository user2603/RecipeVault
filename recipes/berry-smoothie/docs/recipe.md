# Mixed Berry Smoothie

A thick five-minute blend of berries, banana and yogurt.

## Ingredients
- 250g frozen mixed berries
- 1 banana
- 150g natural yogurt
- 200ml milk
- 1 tablespoon honey

## Instructions
1. Drop the frozen berries and banana into a blender.
2. Add the yogurt, milk and honey.
3. Blend until velvety and thick.
4. Pour into tall glasses and drink cold.

## Yield
Makes 2 glasses
