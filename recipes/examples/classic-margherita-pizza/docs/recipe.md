# Classic Margherita Pizza

Two pizzas of soft dough, sweet San Marzano tomatoes and basil.

## Ingredients
- 500g strong flour
- 325ml water
- 7g instant yeast
- 10g salt
- 400g San Marzano tomatoes, crushed
- 250g fresh mozzarella, torn
- Fresh basil
- Olive oil

## Instructions
1. Mix flour, water, yeast and salt; knead 10 minutes and rise 2 hours.
2. Split into two balls and rest 30 minutes.
3. Stretch each ball thin in the middle.
4. Spread a thin layer of tomato, scatter mozzarella, drizzle oil.
5. Bake as hot as your oven goes for 7 minutes and finish with basil.

## Yield
Makes 2 pizzas
