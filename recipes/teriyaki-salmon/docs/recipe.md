# Teriyaki Salmon

Glazed salmon fillets in a sticky soy-mirin lacquer.

## Ingredients
- 2 salmon fillets
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- 3 tablespoons mirin
- 1 tablespoon sake
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- Steamed rice, to serve

## Instructions
1. Warm the soy, mirin, sake and sugar until the sugar dissolves.
2. Sear the salmon skin-side down until crisp.
3. Flip, pour in the sauce and simmer 3 minutes.
4. Spoon the glaze over until lacquered and serve with rice.

## Yield
Serves 2
