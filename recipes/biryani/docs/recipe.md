# Weeknight Chicken Biryani

Spiced rice and chicken steamed together under a saffron lid.

## Ingredients
- 500g basmati rice
- 600g chicken thighs
- 200g yogurt
- 1 tablespoon garam masala
- 1 pinch saffron soaked in warm milk
- 2 onions, fried until crisp
- Whole spices: cardamom, cloves, cinnamon

## Instructions
1. Marinate the chicken in yogurt, garam masala and salt for an hour.
2. Parboil the rice with the whole spices for 4 minutes.
3. Layer the chicken, then half the rice, then saffron milk, then the rest.
4. Seal the pot and steam on the lowest heat for 30 minutes.
5. Rest 10 minutes and fork through the crispy onions.

## Yield
Serves 6
