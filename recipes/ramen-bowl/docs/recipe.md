# Weeknight Ramen Bowl

Rich broth, jammy egg and springy noodles - a full bowl of comfort.

## Ingredients
- 2 nests ramen noodles
- 800ml chicken broth with a spoon of miso
- 2 eggs
- 100g pork belly, sliced
- 2 spring onions, sliced
- 1 sheet nori
- Chili oil

## Instructions
1. Boil the eggs exactly 6.5 minutes and shock in cold water.
2. Warm the broth with the miso whisked in.
3. Sear the pork belly until browned at the edges.
4. Cook the noodles to the packet time and drain hard.
5. Build: noodles, broth, pork, halved eggs, spring onion, nori, chili oil.

## Yield
Serves 2
