# Shakshuka

Eggs poached in a smoky pepper-tomato sauce, straight from the pan.

## Ingredients
- 4 eggs
- 2 cans chopped tomatoes
- 2 peppers, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- Feta and coriander, to finish

## Instructions
1. Soften the onion and peppers in olive oil for 10 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and paprika, then the tomatoes.
3. Simmer until thickened to a jammy sauce.
4. Make wells and crack in the eggs.
5. Cover and cook 6 minutes; finish with feta and coriander.

## Yield
Serves 2
