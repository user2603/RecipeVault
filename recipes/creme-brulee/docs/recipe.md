# Creme Brulee

Vanilla custard under a sheet of torched caramel - the crack is the point.

## Ingredients
- 500ml double cream
- 1 vanilla pod
- 5 egg yolks
- 75g caster sugar
- Demerara, for the top

## Instructions
1. Infuse the cream with the vanilla and warm through.
2. Whisk the yolks and sugar, then temper in the cream.
3. Fill 4 ramekins and bake in a water bath at 140C for 40 minutes.
4. Chill overnight.
5. Sprinkle demerara and torch to a dark amber glass.

## Yield
Makes 4 ramekins
