# Fluffy Weekend Pancakes

Thick, fluffy stacks served with butter and maple syrup.

## Ingredients
- 200g plain flour
- 300ml milk
- 1 egg
- 2 tablespoons melted butter
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- Maple syrup, to serve

## Instructions
1. Whisk the flour, baking powder and sugar.
2. Add the milk, egg and butter and whisk to a smooth batter.
3. Rest the batter for 10 minutes.
4. Cook spoonfuls in a lightly buttered pan until bubbles pop on the surface.
5. Flip and finish the second side, then serve stacked with syrup.

## Yield
Makes about 10 pancakes
