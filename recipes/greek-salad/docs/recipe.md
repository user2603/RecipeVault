# Greek Salad

Cool cucumber, sweet tomato, olives and a slab of feta.

## Ingredients
- 2 cucumbers, chunked
- 4 tomatoes, wedged
- 100g kalamata olives
- 200g feta, in one slab
- Half a red onion, thinly sliced
- Dried oregano and olive oil

## Instructions
1. Toss the cucumber, tomato, onion and olives in a wide bowl.
2. Lay the feta on top in one piece.
3. Dress generously with olive oil and dust with oregano.
4. Serve immediately with bread.

## Yield
Serves 4
