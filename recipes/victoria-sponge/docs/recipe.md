# Victoria Sponge

A light classic sandwich cake, raspberry jam and cream in the middle.

## Ingredients
- 225g softened butter
- 225g caster sugar
- 4 eggs
- 225g self-raising flour
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 5 tablespoons raspberry jam
- 300ml double cream, softly whipped

## Instructions
1. Beat the butter and sugar until pale.
2. Add the eggs one at a time, with the vanilla.
3. Fold in the flour gently.
4. Divide between two tins and bake at 180C for 25 minutes.
5. Cool, then sandwich with jam and cream.

## Yield
8 slices
