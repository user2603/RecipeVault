# Red Wine Beef Stew

Fork-tender chuck braised for hours with root vegetables and thyme.

## Ingredients
- 1kg chuck beef, cubed
- 4 potatoes, chunked
- 4 carrots, chunked
- 1 onion, diced
- 500ml red wine
- 500ml beef stock
- 2 sprigs thyme

## Instructions
1. Sear the beef in batches until dark and set aside.
2. Soften the onion, then add the wine and stock and scrape the pan.
3. Return the beef with the thyme and braise at 150C for 2 hours.
4. Add the potatoes and carrots and cook another 45 minutes.
5. Rest 10 minutes before serving.

## Yield
Serves 6
